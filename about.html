About Me

Hi! I'm Lauren Bock, and I am currently a senior at Iowa State University studying Marketing in the Ivy College of Business. I am originally from Waconia, Minnesota, just west of the Twin Cities. I am excited to be back in Ames for my final year of college and to continue growing both personally and professionally.

I am especially interested in digital marketing, fashion marketing, and social media. I enjoy learning how brands connect with consumers and how creative marketing can help businesses stand out.

One of the most amazing experiences I had the privilege of during college was studying abroad in Florence, Italy. I spent four months living in Florence and had the opportunity to travel throughout Europe, experience different cultures, and meet people from all over the world.

Outside of school, I enjoy spending time with friends, traveling, shopping, and exploring new places. I created this website to share more about my experiences, education, interests, and professional goals.
